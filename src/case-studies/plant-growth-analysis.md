## Introduction

This 13-week study evaluated the effects of different irrigation water sources (rainwater versus groundwater) across four commercial crop species.

## Methods & Tools

We processed raw agricultural sensor data using:
- **R** for statistical execution
- **ggplot2** and **forcats** for plotting growth trends
- **mixed-effects modeling** to account for soil anomalies

## Results

Rainwater irrigation yielded a 14% increase in crop height over groundwater across the test period, with statistically significant variance observed in specific soil types.
